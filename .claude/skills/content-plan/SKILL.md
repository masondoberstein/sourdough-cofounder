---

## Steps

1. Read `brand/brand.md` (posting capacity, goals), `brand/pillars.md`, `content/ideas/ideas.md`, the latest `research/*-trends.md`, and `content/posted/log.md`.
2. Ask one question with a guess: "How many videos can you film this week, and which day is your bake day?" Skip it if she already said.
3. Pick the videos. Mix pillars so the week isn't all one kind. Lean toward formats that did well in the log. Put anything time sensitive first.
4. Batch the filming around her bake day. One bake usually gives several videos (the mix, the shaping, the score, the crumb shot, the mistake). Say which clips to grab from each bake.
5. Suggest posting days and times. Default to the times her own log shows work best. With no data yet, say it's a starting guess and suggest testing.

## Output

Save to `content/plan-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`:

- **Filming list**: grouped by bake, with every shot to grab
- **Posting schedule**: day, platform (TikTok, Reels, or both), video, pillar
- **Scripts to write**: offer to run `/hook-and-script` on each

Keep it to one screen. She should be able to read it while baking.
