## What this does

A weekly scan of the sourdough content world, filtered through her brand so the output is ideas she can film, not a list of other people's videos.

## Steps

1. Read `brand/brand.md`, `brand/pillars.md`, and the last few entries of `content/posted/log.md`.
2. Search the web for recent sourdough and bread content trends. Good queries: "sourdough tiktok trend", "sourdough reels viral", site:tiktok.com sourdough, site:reddit.com/r/Sourdough top this week, "bread tok" news. Use Firecrawl if it's connected, otherwise normal web search. Prefer sources from the last 30 days.
3. Note for each finding: the format (bake with me, POV, mistake, before and after, ASMR, recipe, story), the hook style, any sound or seasonal angle, and the source link.
4. Look at 3 to 5 sourdough creators of a similar size and 2 big ones. What do their top recent videos have in common?
5. Check the calendar: upcoming holidays, seasons, and food days in the next 3 weeks that bread fits (e.g. Thanksgiving rolls, holiday gifting, back to school lunches).

## Output

Save to `research/<YYYY-MM-DD>-trends.md` and show her:

- Top 5 patterns working right now, one line each, with a source link
- 8 to 10 video ideas adapted to her pillars and voice, each with a one line hook
- Anything time sensitive (a trend peaking, a holiday coming)

Add the ideas she likes to `content/ideas/ideas.md`.

## Rules

- Never invent view counts or follower numbers. If a number isn't on the page you read, leave it out.
- Don't tell her to copy a creator's video. Name the pattern, then make it hers.
