## Logging a video

1. Ask for: date, platform, a short title or link, pillar, hook used, and whatever numbers she has. Screenshots of the analytics page work: read the numbers off them.
2. Append one row to the table in `content/posted/log.md`. Only numbers she gave. Leave blanks blank.

## Weekly review (when she asks "what's working" or at the end of a week)

1. Read the whole log.
2. Compare by pillar, hook style, length, posting day and time, and platform. Saves and shares count more than likes.
3. Give her 3 findings, each with the rows that back it up, and 1 thing to try next week.
4. If there are fewer than about 10 posts, say the patterns are early guesses.
5. Add what she wants to keep doing to `brand/pillars.md` or `brand/voice.md`.
