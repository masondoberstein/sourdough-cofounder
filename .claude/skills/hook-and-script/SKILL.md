## Steps

1. Read `brand/voice.md` and `brand/pillars.md`. Check `content/posted/log.md` for hooks that worked before.
2. If the idea is vague, ask one question with a guess (what's the payoff the viewer gets?).
3. Write **3 hook options**, each a different style:
   - Visual: what's on screen in the first second (the crumb, the oven spring, a fail)
   - Statement: a specific claim, number, or mistake ("You're killing your starter with tap water")
   - Story or POV: "POV: your first loaf actually rose"
   Each hook is under 10 words spoken, and works with the sound off as on screen text.
4. Write the script in her voice, beat by beat with rough seconds:
   - 0 to 2s hook
   - the payoff or steps, one idea per beat
   - a loop or a reason to watch twice if it fits
   - a soft CTA that matches the goal (follow for part 2, comment your question, save this for bake day)
5. Shot list: every clip she needs, with angle (overhead, side, close up) and whether it needs voiceover or live sound.
6. Caption: run `/captions` or write a short one inline.
7. Run the humanizer rules over the spoken lines so they sound like her talking.

## Output

Save to `content/scripts/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md` and show the 3 hooks first, then the script, shot list and caption.

## Rules

- Baking facts must be correct (temps, times, hydration). Say so if something depends on her kitchen.
- No clickbait she can't pay off in the video.
